# The Ballad of Linner: A Pointless Journey

In the vast, single-color void of the Vectorverse, there exists only one fundamental truth: The Line. 

For eons, The Line was perfectly flat. It was managed by the Bureau of Sharp Angles, a bureaucratic nightmare of Rectangles and Triangles who hated two things: fun, and anything that wasn't a polygon. 

Enter our hero: **Linner**. 

Linner is a circle. This immediately makes him a prime suspect in the eyes of the Bureau. Worse, Linner has a single, rebellious ray trapped inside his perfectly round body. Some say it's a navigational compass. Others say it's a structural defect. Linner just calls it his "steering wheel," and it makes him incredibly dizzy when he gets going fast.

One day, Linner looked at the endless, flat brown and green Line and said, "This is boring. I want to go *up*." 

By sheer force of will, Linner began to warp the very fabric of the ground. The Line began to rise, fall, and twist. The Bureau of Sharp Angles was furious. They dispatched their finest enforcers—heavily armed Triangles with edgy personalities and Rectangles who are strictly by-the-book—to stop him. 

But Linner isn't just a rolling ball of defiance. He's armed. By twitching his internal ray up and down, Linner can fire high-velocity geometric retorts in a perfect 180-degree arc (in strict 10-degree increments, because he's still a creature of mathematics, after all). 

To stop him, the Bureau hasn't just sent goons; they've sabotaged The Line itself. Suddenly, solid ground gives way to terrifying abysses. Even worse, they've installed "Water Lines" (which are blue, and everyone knows circles can't swim; they just get soggy and lose their structural integrity). 

Linner must jump, shoot, and accelerate through five different gears of pure, unadulterated rolling power. To survive the hardest sectors, he'll need to hunt down the legendary Rhombus Powerups—mystical, shiny shapes that the Bureau couldn't classify. Snagging one allows Linner to either break the speed limit or "Phase Out" entirely, becoming a ghost to the oppressive geometry around him.

The clock is ticking. The Line is moving. The Rectangles are coming. 

Can you guide Linner to the end of the Line before the timer runs out? Can you etch your 5-character name into the magnetic disk of history? 

Roll fast. Shoot sharp. Stay well-rounded.
